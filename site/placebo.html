The full method and the rest of the results

The three conditions

Cold. Nothing at all, the baseline almost everyone stops at.

Mine. My real mined profile, 10,511 bytes, built from 1,843 sessions and 1.74M tokens of my own work.

The fake. An invented editorial designer, confident and opinionated, and deliberately silent on every single thing the run measured. Length matched at 10,545 bytes against 10,511, and the harness refuses to start if that gap goes past five per cent.

The pass rule, fixed before the first run

An effect counts only if the two groups of five do not overlap at all. For a prediction that names a direction, that happens by chance about once in 252 orderings. A prediction is supported only if it separates on at least two of three tasks, against cold and against the fake.

What survives, because leaving it out would be its own dishonesty

The profile does change the output. The saturation prediction met its leg against cold, separated, in the direction stated in advance, on two of three tasks. What got no support is that the change comes from what is inside it rather than from its length.

Why the run before this one did not count

The metrics were chosen after seeing the outputs, so they got measured exactly where the gap already was. And there was no fake, so ten kilobytes of confident text could not be told apart from ten kilobytes of mine. Both are why this one was written down in advance.
