## Deliberately absent

No client names, logos, testimonials, case studies, outcome metrics, company entity claim, security or compliance promise, error reduction percentage, production readiness claim, payment checkout, and no claim that Emulo understands a team's codebase automatically. The hero figure is an illustration and is labelled as one; no chart on the page plots real repository data.
