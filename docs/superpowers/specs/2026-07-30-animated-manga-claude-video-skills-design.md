# Animated Manga Claude Video Skills Reel

Date: 2026-07-30
Status: Approved design, awaiting written-spec review

## Goal

Create one 25–30 second vertical Reel/Short that proves Claude can make polished videos when it has the right video skills.

The reel must:

- tease two skills without saying or showing the names Remotion or HyperFrames;
- use Ohad's cloned voice;
- use a cinematic animated manga/comic language for the entire reel;
- show real BiosRios video output as proof;
- end with: `Comment "SKILL" and I'll send you the exact setup.`;
- feel fast, addictive, premium, and clean rather than chaotic or template-made.

The first prototype excludes a face clone. A face clone may be tested in a later episode only if it passes a strict likeness and realism review.

## Audience and Platform

- Primary platform: Instagram Reels
- Reusable on: YouTube Shorts and X
- Canvas: 9:16
- Working master: 2160 × 3840 when source assets support it
- Delivery encode: platform-safe H.264 MP4, with a 1080 × 1920 derivative if required
- Duration target: 27 seconds, acceptable range 25–30 seconds

## Creative Thesis

This is not a clean editorial video with manga decorations. The video itself behaves like a moving comic.

The viewer travels through hand-drawn panels, ink reveals, halftone shadows, speed lines, animated character poses, and camera moves. Real product footage appears inside comic panels as evidence. The drawing style supplies identity; the real footage supplies trust.

The BiosRios white manga character is the recurring authorial signature. It acts in the scene rather than sitting in a fixed corner.

## Script

The script is guided by the active Emulo writing profile:

> Claude can make videos like this. But it needs the right skills. One turns code into frame-perfect animation. The other makes Claude direct the whole thing: script, storyboard, motion, captions, sound, and the final render. I used both to make the reel you're watching right now. Comment "SKILL" and I'll send you the exact setup.

Rules:

- Do not say or display `Remotion` or `HyperFrames`.
- Do not add unsupported claims or fake metrics.
- Keep normal capitalization.
- Do not use em or en dashes.
- The cloned voice determines the final edit duration. Visual timings conform to the real voice track.

## Visual System

### Palette

- Paper: warm off-white
- Ink: near-black
- Accent: BiosRios coral
- Optional gray: halftone and secondary ink only

No purple, glossy gradients, 3D emoji, glass cards, or generic AI-generated decoration.

### Drawing Language

- Crisp high-resolution manga line art
- Controlled cross-hatching and halftone texture
- Hand-drawn panel borders
- Impact frames and speed lines used only on actual story beats
- Real UI and footage remain legible and are not damaged by heavy filters
- Character proportions and facial identity remain consistent across all poses

### Typography

- Heavy condensed sans for primary captions
- Hand-lettered comic display face for impact words only
- Monospace for genuine code
- Two to five words per kinetic caption
- Captions never cover the proof object or the character's face

## Motion System

- Meaningful visual response every 0.7–1.3 seconds
- Full scene or visual-world change every 2–3 seconds
- Object-driven transitions: panel push, ink wipe, match cut, camera travel, page split
- No generic slideshow wipes
- No idle character holds
- No full-frame shake except one subtle CTA paper impact
- Motion remains seek-safe and deterministic
- The viewer must be able to understand every proof shot at normal playback speed

The dopamine comes from anticipation, transformation, and payoff. It must not come from clutter or random movement.

## Audio System

- Ohad's cloned voice is the timing master and remains the loudest element.
- Music: tight, modern pulse that supports a comic-action rhythm without sounding like generic anime trailer music.
- Real SFX only: paper swish, ink scratch, panel impact, UI click, notification hit, subtle riser.
- One meaningful cue roughly every 1.5–2.5 seconds, never the same sample twice in a row.
- SFX attach to visible causes.
- Final mix targets platform-safe loudness with clear speech and no clipping.

## Asset Policy

Use:

- the existing polished white BiosRios character set;
- real BiosRios renders as proof;
- real code and real product UI;
- the existing cloned-voice workflow;
- newly authored manga backgrounds, panel layouts, and motion assets at sufficient resolution.

Do not use:

- fake product UI;
- unrelated stock anime characters;
- inconsistent AI-generated variants of the BiosRios character;
- a face clone in this prototype;
- the names or logos of the two teased skills.

## Implementation Shape

The reel is a seven-scene composition driven by one real voice track.

Each scene owns:

- one manga page or panel layout;
- its character pose and expression;
- real proof media where required;
- kinetic-caption phrases;
- deterministic motion timing;
- named audio cues.

Scenes are assembled into one 9:16 master timeline. The voice track establishes scene boundaries. Captions and visual actions are aligned to word-level timings.

## Failure Handling

- If the cloned voice is unclear, regenerate only the affected line before animation timing is finalized.
- If a generated character pose drifts from the approved identity, reject it and use an existing polished pose.
- If a proof surface cannot be captured honestly, restructure the scene around available real output rather than drawing fake UI.
- If 4K source generation is unavailable, keep the 2160 × 3840 composition but do not upscale low-resolution proof footage past a visibly soft limit.
- If pace harms readability, remove a visual action rather than extending the reel beyond 30 seconds.

## Verification

The prototype is not complete until:

- the rendered file decodes from start to finish;
- actual duration is within 25–30 seconds;
- the names Remotion and HyperFrames do not appear in voice, captions, UI crops, metadata overlays, or the CTA;
- the cloned voice is clear and synchronized;
- each spoken noun has a matching visible action or proof object;
- no caption collides with another caption, the character's face, or critical UI;
- no unintended freeze lasts 0.7 seconds or longer;
- real footage moves inside its comic panels;
- the final CTA remains readable for at least 1.2 seconds;
- the contact sheet and the full rendered video are both inspected;
- the result looks clean at phone size, not only on a desktop preview.
