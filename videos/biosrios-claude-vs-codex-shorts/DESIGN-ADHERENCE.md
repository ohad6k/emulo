# Shorts Design Adherence

- 1080x1920 safe vertical layouts: implemented.
- Warm paper, black ink, proof red, Claude orange, Codex blue: implemented.
- Premium hybrid gradients, dimensional 3D metaphors, proof cards, caption
  pills, restrained depth, and keyword CTAs: implemented.

- No Short declares a model winner.
- `0 TOKENS`, `160,649 INPUT`, and `0 FILES CHANGED` are shown only with their
