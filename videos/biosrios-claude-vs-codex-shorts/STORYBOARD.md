## Shared edit rules

- Editorial-metaphor hook, proof-led middle, keyword-CTA close.
- Change the visual question every 1.5-2.5 seconds, but hold each receipt long
  enough to read.
- Keep evidence above the center safe line and captions clear of platform UI.
- Use one highlighted word or number at a time.
- BiosRios character anchors support the setup without covering proof.
- Re-typeset evidence must be labeled `VERIFIED LOCAL EXCERPT` and retain its
  exact source/line reference. Never describe a designed excerpt as a raw
  screenshot.
- The 2026-07-29 reference audit informed the warm gradients, 3D metaphors,
  proof cards, caption pills, depth, and keyword CTA hierarchy.
